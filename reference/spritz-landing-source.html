The Business Case

A clean space is a business asset.

Your environment signals everything before anyone speaks. Clients, tenants, and patients decide within seconds — and that decision compounds every visit.

Cleanliness compounds.

The difference between great and average cleaning isn't visible on day one — it's visible on day ninety. Buildup, wear, and drift quietly erode the way your space feels. Recurring maintenance prevents it before it starts.

90d
is when irregular cleaning becomes visibly obvious to clients and tenants
01

Creates a strong first impression

Clients form opinions about your business in the time it takes them to walk to your reception. A consistently spotless space sets the tone before a single word is spoken.

02

Supports a professional environment

Staff perform measurably better in well-maintained spaces. Cleanliness is a direct input to morale, focus, and the kind of culture that retains people.

03

Helps spaces stay presentable

Irregular cleaning lets dirt, scuffs, and buildup accumulate in places that take hours of deep work to undo. Recurring maintenance keeps the baseline high — permanently.

04

Keeps operations running smoothly

No distractions, no complaints from staff or tenants, no last-minute scrambles before an important visit. Cleaning is one thing that should never be a fire to put out.

Common Questions

The answers you need before you call.

Most of our prospects ask the same six questions. Here are the answers — straightforward, no salesy hedging.

How long is the contract?

Standard recurring contracts are 12 months with a 30-day notice clause. We don't lock people in — if it's not working, you can leave. We just ask for fair notice so we can plan around it.

What's the minimum monthly commitment?

Our recurring contracts start at $1,500 per month. This ensures we can dedicate consistent crews and quality infrastructure to your space. Below that threshold we'd be cutting corners — and that's not what Spritz does.

Are you insured and bonded?

Yes. Full commercial liability insurance and bonding on every member of our team. Certificates available on request before any contract is signed.

Do you provide your own supplies and equipment?

Yes — all standard supplies, equipment, and eco-conscious products are included. Specialty consumables (paper goods, soap refills for your dispensers) can be added to the contract or sourced separately, depending on your preference.

What if I'm not happy with a visit?

Flag it within 24 hours and we send a crew back to fix it — no charge. Our quality check reports also catch most issues before you do. We're not interested in a one-and-done relationship; we're interested in keeping you as a client for years.

How fast can you start?

From first contact to first visit, we typically need 7–14 days. The walkthrough usually happens within 5 business days, contract turnaround is 2–3 days, and we book your first service shortly after. Urgent timelines can be accommodated — just tell us when you need to start.
